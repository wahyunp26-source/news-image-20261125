A single 1024×1024 image summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026: two oil
tankers hit mines and burn in the Strait of Hormuz, Pakistan's first
astronaut docks at Tiangong, a Cairo fuel-tanker fire kills 30, plus 26 more
verified stories from the day — a UTC timeline, a numbers panel, a dotted
world map with numbered pins, and a date check that names the big stories
left out because they happened earlier (Dell, Huawei, Sinner — all 24 Nov).

Pure Python standard library. No network, no downloads, deterministic:
two runs produce a byte-identical `entry.png`. Fonts are rendered into a
compressed glyph-atlas blob (`assets_blob.py`) at build time only.

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py`
